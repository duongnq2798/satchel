Satchel
NEAR Chain Signatures · NEP-171 NFT · live on testnet and mainnet

Sell a wallet.Prove nothing stayed behind.

A satchel is a NEAR NFT that controls one address on 16 EVM chains, signed for by NEAR's MPC network. Transfer the NFT and the wallet moves with it. The contract keeps a public record of everything it signed, so a buyer can check what the seller could have kept back before paying.
