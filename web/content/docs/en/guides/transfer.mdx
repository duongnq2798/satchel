A transfer is a plain NEP-171 `nft_transfer`: no payment, no listing. It moves the satchel to another NEAR
account, and with it the only right to have the contract sign for the satchel's address. Everything the
address holds on all 16 chains changes hands in that one NEAR transaction, and nothing on the EVM chains
moves.

<Callout type="danger" title="A transfer can't be undone">
Only the new owner can send the satchel back. On mainnet, the address may hold real funds; check the
receiver's account name character by character.
</Callout>

## What you need

- A satchel you own, **not listed**. The app shows **Transfer the NFT** only on a satchel that isn't for
  sale; [delist it](/docs/guides/sell#delist) first if it is.
- The receiver's NEAR account on the same network: `friend.testnet` on testnet, `friend.near` on mainnet.
- Your NEAR wallet, connected ([Connect a NEAR wallet](/docs/guides/wallets)).

## Transfer

<Steps>

1. **Open the satchel's page** (`/satchel/<id>`, or `/mainnet/satchel/<id>`) and find **Sell or transfer**.

2. **Enter the receiver's account** in the field that reads `Receiver, like friend.testnet` (or
   `friend.near` on mainnet), and click **Transfer the NFT**.

3. **The app checks the receiver** before your wallet opens. See
   [the receiver check](#the-receiver-check).

4. **Approve `nft_transfer` in your wallet**: 1 yoctoNEAR, 30 TGas. Its arguments are the token id and the
   receiver; check the receiver once more there.

5. **Done.** The page reads again and links to the transaction. The satchel now shows its new owner, and
   it no longer appears in your satchels.

</Steps>

## The receiver check

The contract accepts any account name, including one that doesn't exist yet. An NFT sent to such a name is
lost: no one may ever own that name, or anyone could register it later and take the satchel with everything
its address holds. So before it signs, the app refuses:

| The app says | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `Enter the receiver's NEAR account, like friend.testnet` | The name isn't a valid NEAR account id |
| `v1.satchel-nft.testnet can never transfer a satchel out` | The receiver is the Satchel contract or the MPC contract, which can never send it on |
| `bob.testnet doesn't exist on NEAR testnet, so a satchel sent there could be lost. Ask for an account that exists.` | No such account on this network. A `.near` name on testnet, or a typo, ends here |
| `Couldn't check that bob.testnet exists, so nothing was signed: …` | The NEAR RPC didn't answer. Try again |

<Callout type="warning" title="Transfers made elsewhere get no check">
The check runs in the app and in the `satchel-nft` CLI. A transfer made another way, with the near CLI or
another wallet or marketplace, calls the contract directly, and the contract takes any name.
</Callout>

## What moves, and what stays

| | After the transfer |
| --- | --- |
| The NFT | Owned by the receiver |
| The address | The same `0x` address on all 16 chains: it comes from the path `satchel/<id>`, which never changes |
| The right to sign | The receiver's alone. Your next `sign_evm` is refused: `Satchel: only the token's owner can do this` |
| The coins and tokens at the address | Untouched, now controlled by the receiver |
| The signing record | Unchanged. It belongs to the token and only grows; the receiver inherits it |
| Any listing | Ended. Every transfer path removes the token's listing |

Because the record stays with the token, a satchel you give away carries its history. If you are on the
receiving end of a satchel, read its report at [Verify](/verify) first, as you would before buying.

## In a terminal

```bash
npx -y satchel-nft@0.2.0 transfer 7 friend.testnet --as alice.testnet
```

Use your own satchel's id and your own account for `--as`: as written, it is refused, because
`alice.testnet` doesn't own satchel #7.

It refuses an account that doesn't own the satchel, then runs the same receiver check, then shows what it
will sign:

```text
Gives satchel #7, and whatever its address holds on every chain, to friend.testnet.
```

After you type `yes`, it prints `Sent satchel #7 to friend.testnet:` and the transaction's link. A refused
receiver exits with code 1 and nothing signed. The [CLI reference](/docs/developers/cli) has every option.
